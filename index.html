📲 Install on Android Phone

Quick solutions for "App cannot be installed"
Why does Chrome say "App cannot be installed"? Android Chrome requires an encrypted public HTTPS connection to automatically mint a WebAPK package. Over local Wi-Fi (http://192.168.x.x), Google's remote minting server cannot reach a private IP.
✅ Instant 5-Second Fix:
  1. In Chrome on your phone, tap the three dots (⋮) at top-right.
  2. Tap "Add to Home screen".
  3. Tap "Add" / "Add shortcut".
🎉 Done! The timetable app icon is now on your phone home screen and launches full screen offline!
Alternative: Enable Local Install in Chrome
On your phone Chrome, go to:
chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure
Add your laptop URL, enable it, and relaunch Chrome.
